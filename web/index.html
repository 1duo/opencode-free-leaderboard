Each score uses the largest complete current panel. Question counts, evaluation dates, and 95% sampling intervals show how much evidence supports it. These benchmarks do not measure everyday agent performance.

Benchmark details

Full screens use 20 GPQA Diamond, 20 LiveBench spatial / zebra questions (2024-11-25), and 40 LiveCodeBench Python problems (release_v6). The practical Python subset uses BigCodeBench-Instruct v0.1.4: 12 standard-library and 12 scientific Python tasks, with full upstream tests. These are custom subsets, not official benchmark scores. Intervals use 10,000 stratified bootstrap resamples and measure question sampling, not variation between repeated answers.

All models use their highest selectable reasoning effort, subject to a 4,096-token combined cap. “Provider-managed” means no selectable level. Answers are generated through OpenCode without permitted tools or repair turns. Client versions, earlier evaluations, and execution records are available in the exports and source.

Community submissions are labeled with their contributor and linked audit file. CI checks complete panel coverage and recomputes scores; private answers and provider behavior have not been independently reproduced.
