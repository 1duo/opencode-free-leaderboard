How to read the scores

Reasoning

Equal average of GPQA and LiveBench. Models tested only on LiveBench are labeled accordingly.

Coding

LiveCodeBench Python pass@1: the share of problems solved with one accepted answer.

Each score uses the largest complete current panel. Question counts, evaluation dates, and 95% sampling intervals show how much evidence supports it. These benchmarks do not measure everyday agent performance.

Benchmark details

Full screens use 20 GPQA Diamond, 20 LiveBench spatial / zebra questions (2024-11-25), and 40 LiveCodeBench Python problems (release_v6). These are custom subsets. Intervals use 10,000 stratified bootstrap resamples and measure question sampling, not variation between repeated answers.

All models use their highest selectable reasoning effort, subject to a 4,096-token combined cap. “Provider-managed” means no selectable level. Answers are generated through OpenCode without permitted tools or repair turns. Client versions, earlier evaluations, and execution records are available in the exports and source.
